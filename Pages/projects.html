The following project areas demonstrate the types of agricultural support that Lilitha's Nature Relief focuses on.

Smart Irrigation

Smart irrigation uses technology-based approaches to support water management. The purpose is to help agricultural businesses plan water use more effectively.

Drone Crop Monitoring

Drone technology can assist with observing crop conditions over agricultural areas. Monitoring information can help identify areas that may require attention.

Soil Testing

Soil testing provides information about soil conditions and can support agricultural planning and decision-making.

Greenhouse Management

Greenhouse management supports agricultural activities carried out in controlled environments.

Crop Health Monitoring

Crop health monitoring focuses on observing crops and identifying possible problems at an early stage.

Small-Scale Farmer Support

Small-scale agricultural businesses may require practical and affordable support. Consultation allows clients to explain their specific needs.
