About Us

Learn more about Lilitha's Nature Relief, our purpose and the agricultural support we provide.

Who We Are

Lilitha's Nature Relief is a local South African organisation that provides practical and technology-based support to farmers and agricultural businesses.

The organisation aims to make agricultural support easier to access by providing services that can assist clients with agricultural planning, crop care, soil information, greenhouse management and technology-based solutions.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide affordable, practical and technology-based agricultural support that helps farmers and agricultural businesses manage their activities more effectively.

Our Vision

Our vision is to make agricultural support easier to access across South Africa by combining practical knowledge, technology and sustainable approaches.

Who We Support

Our services are intended for subsistence farmers, upcoming farmers, small agricultural businesses and other agricultural organisations that require practical support.

Where We Operate

Lilitha's Nature Relief provides services in areas including the Eastern Cape and Gauteng, South Africa. Clients can use the consultation page to provide their province and explain the type of agricultural support they require.
