About the organisation

Helping people make farming a little easier.

Lilitha's Nature Relief is a local South African company that uses technology to support farming.

Who we are

A new company with a practical purpose.

Lilitha's Nature Relief started late last year and was created to help upcoming farmers, people who do subsistence farming and companies that need support with their farming activities.

We offer services such as soil testing, crop care and greenhouse support, while focusing on affordable and environmentally friendly approaches.

Our purpose

To help farmers access useful agricultural support, reduce unnecessary costs and make better use of technology.

Lilitha's Nature Relief

Using technology to make farming easier, more affordable and more environmentally friendly for farmers in South Africa.
